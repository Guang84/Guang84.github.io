Technology

GLab explores, applies and develops technology through practical engineering, careful testing and attention to security, maintainability and real-world needs.

The perspective

Technology should solve real problems

GLab approaches technology as a practical discipline: understand the need, choose an appropriate method, build with care, test against real requirements and explain what is known. New tools are useful when they improve a task, reduce avoidable complexity or make a system more dependable.

Technical capability matters, but so do maintainability, security, privacy, cost, accessibility and the people who must operate the result.

What we practise

Engineering across software, networks and systems

Software & data

Web applications, operational workflows, information design, structured data and automation.

Networks & infrastructure

ISP operations, broadband systems, network visibility, GIS, fibre planning and device monitoring.

Security & resilience

Authorised diagnostics, least-privilege access, privacy-aware development, Linux systems and recovery-minded operations.

Emerging technologies

AI, machine learning and measurement systems explored according to their practical value, risks and verifiable limits.

Engineering standards

Build, test, document and maintain

GLab aims to make technical work understandable and maintainable. Project records should state the intended use, dependencies, validation performed, known limitations and current lifecycle status. A demonstration is not proof of production readiness, and a planned feature is not a delivered capability.

We favour established tools where they fit, keep security boundaries explicit and avoid collecting or publishing information that is not needed.
