GLab is a working collection of projects, guides, and notes about why a project exists, how it develops, what has been learned, and what still needs work. Finished work, experiments, and retired systems are described differently so readers can tell what is ready to use.

I’m passionate about building a future where technology and nature coexist harmoniously. I try to choose tools for the problem they solve, how well they can be maintained, and how they affect the people and places around them.
