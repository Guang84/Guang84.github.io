GLabNetOps

GLabNetOps is our ISP operations and network management product. It brings customer and service workflows together with NetVision mapping, fibre planning, device monitoring and staff administration.

The product is in development. Its public page is an overview, not a live-service login. Hardware compatibility depends on local, model-specific validation.

How we work

Define it. Build it. Test it. Maintain it.

We work to an agreed scope and record the limits that matter to an operator.

  1. 01

    Understand the job

    Identify users, the operating environment and the actual problem.

  2. 02

    Agree the scope

    Set deliverables, access permissions, acceptance checks and exclusions.

  3. 03

    Build and test

    Check the work in a relevant environment and document what remains uncertain.

  4. 04

    Maintain or retire

    Prefer repair and reuse when practical. Make environmental claims only when we have evidence.
