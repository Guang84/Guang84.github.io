Innovation

GLab explores ideas and develops practical solutions through research, experimentation, prototypes and evidence-led improvement.

The perspective

Innovation starts with a useful question

For GLab, innovation is not simply novelty. It is the process of identifying a real need, exploring a better approach and determining whether that approach works in the conditions where it will be used.

Ideas deserve room to be explored, but they should not be presented as proven solutions before they have been tested.

From idea to implementation

Explore, experiment, verify and improve

01

Question

Define the problem, user, context and intended outcome.

02

Research

Review existing approaches, evidence, dependencies and risks.

03

Prototype

Build a limited test that makes assumptions visible.

04

Validate

Test the relevant behaviour and document failures and limits.

05

Develop

Improve, release or stop based on evidence and practical value.

How we report progress

Distinguish ideas, prototypes and products

GLab's project records distinguish concepts, research, active development, prototypes, testing, releases and archived work. This makes it easier to understand what exists today, what is being explored and what still needs validation.

Innovation should be useful, responsible and open to correction. Where an idea does not work, that is part of the development record—not a result to hide.
