# GLab editorial voice and page tone

GLab's overarching voice is **thoughtful, confident, precise, responsible and forward-looking**. It values substance over promotion, accuracy over exaggeration and meaningful progress over appearances.

| Area | Editorial tone |
| --- | --- |
| Homepage and product pages | Confident, precise and slightly elevated |
| Nature | Reflective, respectful and purposeful |
| Technology | Analytical, knowledgeable and technically precise |
| Innovation | Curious, exploratory and ambitious without hype |
| Projects | Factual, transparent and specific about lifecycle status |
| Insights | Authoritative, educational and evidence-aware |
| About | Professional, clear and grounded in the enterprise's actual work |
| Business and support | Direct, credible, practical and empathetic |
| Errors and limitations | Calm, transparent and accountable |
| Campaigns | Energetic and benefit-focused without unsupported claims |

## Writing rules

- Use British English consistently.
- Prefer concrete explanations to slogans and generic corporate language.
- Distinguish principles, intentions, current practices, completed work and measurable outcomes.
- Never describe a prototype as a production-ready product.
- Do not imply that AI assistance, research or planning proves a feature has shipped.
- Keep technical terminology accurate and explain uncommon terms for the intended reader.
- Avoid repeated introductions, filler, inflated adjectives and unsubstantiated claims.
- Make limitations, uncertainty, dependencies and next steps visible where they matter.
