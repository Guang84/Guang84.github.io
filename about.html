About

Hi, I’m Guang

My background combines hands-on broadband and network operations with self-directed software, Linux, cybersecurity, automation and community-focused technology projects.

Technology should do more than innovate. It should improve lives, strengthen communities, and help build a sustainable future.

What GLab is

A personal profile and public knowledge hub.

GLab connects the source repositories I maintain with the stories behind them: project case studies, technical articles, practical guides, experiments and lessons learned. It is designed so visitors can understand the work without digging through repository internals first.
