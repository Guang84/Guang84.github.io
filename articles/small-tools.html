In praise of small, understandable tools

A note about software that can be held in one person’s head—and why constraint can be a form of care.

Published 2026-06-03Updated 2026-09-04
  • Software
  • Maintenance
  • Sustainable web

Observation

Complexity has a maintenance cost.

Every dependency makes a promise about the future. Sometimes that trade is worthwhile. Often a plain file, a short script, or a static page will live longer and ask less of everyone who inherits it.

Practice

Choose the smallest durable thing.

  • Prefer formats people can open without special tools
  • Keep the deployment path boring
  • Document the reason behind constraints
  • Leave room for the next maintainer to understand the whole
