Responsible AI begins before the model

A practical framework for deciding whether an AI system should exist, what it should optimize, and who gets to question it.

Published 2026-07-11Updated 2026-09-04
  • Responsible AI
  • Design
  • Governance

Premise

The first design decision is whether to build.

Responsible development does not start at model selection. It starts with the people affected, the existing process, and an honest account of what automation might erase as well as improve.

OECD AI Principles ↗

Framework

Four questions before a prototype.

  • Who experiences the benefit, and who absorbs the risk?
  • Can a simpler rule or interface solve the problem?
  • What evidence would cause us to stop?
  • Can an affected person understand and contest the output?
NIST AI RMF ↗UNESCO AI ethics recommendation ↗

Practice

Treat limits as product features.

Uncertainty, provenance, correction, and refusal should be designed into the main experience. They are not disclaimers to add after a demo succeeds.
