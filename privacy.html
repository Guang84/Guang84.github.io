Privacy and data use

Privacy notice

Last updated: November 4, 2026.

This notice explains the information that may be processed when you visit the public GLab Enterprise website at guang84.github.io or contact GLab Ent. through the channels linked here. It describes this public website only. Separate GLab products, hosted software, client environments, project engagements and SaaS services require their own product- or service-specific privacy information where personal data is processed.

Information handled by this website

Hosting and security records. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that visits to GitHub Pages sites involve logging and storage of visitor IP addresses for security purposes. Hosting infrastructure may also process request details such as the requested page, request time, browser and device information. GLab does not operate or control GitHub's hosting logs.

Browser preferences and offline files. The site may store interface preferences such as theme, text size and colour palette in browser storage. Its service worker can cache website files on your device to support offline access. These browser-side records are not a GLab website account or customer database. You can clear them through your browser's site-data controls; doing so may remove saved preferences and offline files.

Fonts and advertising. Pages request fonts from Google Fonts. Google AdSense advertising code is enabled on selected public pages, including the homepage and publication or project content. When those third-party resources load, their providers may receive technical request information such as IP address, browser/device details, page URL, cookies or similar identifiers.

Direct messages. The website links to email and WhatsApp rather than collecting messages through a first-party website form. When you choose to contact GLab Ent., the relevant provider processes your email address or phone/account identifier, message content and any attachments you choose to send. GLab uses the information to review and respond to the enquiry, manage related work, maintain appropriate business records and address follow-up matters.

The public website does not currently provide visitor registration, user accounts, a comment system or an embedded checkout/payment form. The official business-registration verification link opens an external government service. A UPI transfer, if separately initiated by you through a bank or payment application, is handled by that payment provider; do not send GLab payment PINs, OTPs, passwords or banking credentials.

Why information may be processed

Information described in this notice may be used to deliver and secure the website, remember your optional interface settings, provide offline access, load fonts and advertising, respond to messages you initiate, keep necessary business correspondence, and meet applicable legal or regulatory requirements. GLab does not use this public website to create visitor profiles or operate a customer account system.

Third-party services and advertising

GitHub provides hosting and repository services. Google Fonts supplies typefaces. Google AdSense and its advertising partners provide, measure and protect advertising and may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or other identifiers. Advertising personalisation and consent options depend on the provider's configuration, your choices, applicable requirements and the settings available for your device or account. GLab cannot control the independent data practices of these providers.

For more information, review Google's information about advertising technologies, how Google uses information from partner sites and apps, and Google AdSense cookie information. You can review advertising settings through Google My Ad Center. Browser settings may also allow you to restrict or clear cookies, although blocking some resources may affect site functions.

Consent controls and advertising limitations

The current public website does not include a separate first-party cookie banner or on-site consent-preference centre. Browser settings and Google advertising settings can control some storage or advertising choices, but this notice is not itself a consent mechanism and clearing local browser storage does not necessarily withdraw a choice previously recorded by an external provider. Google requires AdSense publishers serving personalised ads to visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland to use a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. The current repository review did not identify such a platform in the website code. The live AdSense configuration and visitor regions should therefore be checked, and an appropriate certified consent flow configured before personalised ads are served to audiences for which Google or applicable law requires it.

Storage and retention

Browser preferences and cached files remain on your device until they expire or you clear the relevant site data. Messages sent to GLab may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, complete related work, maintain business and accounting records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Email, WhatsApp, hosting, advertising and other providers apply their own retention practices under their terms and policies. GLab does not control provider-side retention.

External links and verification

This website links to GitHub, WhatsApp, the official business-registration verification portal, Google services and other external websites. When you follow an external link, that provider's privacy notice and terms apply to your interaction there. Review them before submitting information. The presence of a link does not mean GLab controls or endorses every practice of the external provider.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose not to contact GLab, adjust browser storage and cookie settings, clear offline site data, or manage Google advertising preferences. Where applicable data-protection law provides rights to request access, correction, erasure or other action regarding personal information that you have sent directly to GLab, email he.3.glab.verse@gmail.com with the relevant details. GLab may need to verify the request and may retain information where a lawful record-keeping or other permitted ground applies. Requests concerning data held by GitHub, Google, WhatsApp, email providers or payment providers should also be directed to those providers.

Security and sensitive information

GLab takes reasonable care with communications under its control, but no website, email service, messaging platform or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not send passwords, one-time passcodes, private keys, payment credentials, customer lists, unredacted network diagrams or confidential infrastructure details through public enquiries.

Changes and contact

This notice may be revised when website features, providers or applicable requirements change. The revision date appears at the top of this page. Questions, privacy concerns and requests about information sent directly to GLab can be addressed to he.3.glab.verse@gmail.com.
